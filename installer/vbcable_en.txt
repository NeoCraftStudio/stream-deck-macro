SOUND INTO YOUR MICROPHONE (optional)

For a key's sound to reach Discord or a game alongside your voice, Windows
needs a virtual audio cable. It is free and you install it once.

NeoCraft Macro Desk handles the rest on its own: it detects the cable, mixes
the sound with your microphone and configures itself. All you do is pick the
cable as your microphone in Discord.

Download VB-CABLE from:
  https://vb-audio.com/Cable/

Why it is not bundled with this installer: VB-CABLE belongs to another
company, and redistributing their software requires a paid licence. Linking
is free -- and this way you download straight from the official source.

Without it, everything else in the program works normally. Only sending
sound to your microphone is unavailable.
